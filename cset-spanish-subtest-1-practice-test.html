Study Tips for Subtest I

Use these alongside your practice questions.

📖

Read the Official SMRs

Download the CSET Spanish Subject Matter Requirements from ctcexams.nesinc.com to see exactly how General Linguistics and Linguistics of the Target Language are defined.

🧠

Learn Core Linguistic Terminology

Know terms like phoneme, morpheme, syntax, and pragmatics cold — General Linguistics questions often hinge on precise terminology, not just Spanish examples.

📰

Analyze Authentic Spanish Sentences

Practice applying grammar and lexical concepts — subjunctive triggers, ser/estar, register — to real sentences, not just isolated rules.

❌

Study False Cognates & Lexical Nuance

Learn embarazado, sensible, librería, éxito, and others — false cognates and precise word meaning are part of Linguistics of the Target Language.

🎙

Review Language Acquisition Theory

Concepts like Krashen's Input Hypothesis and the Affective Filter fall under General Linguistics' language-acquisition strand — review the core ideas, not just the labels.

✍️

Practice Written Responses

The real exam includes three short constructed-response questions. Practice writing short, focused responses in Spanish that demonstrate linguistic analysis.
