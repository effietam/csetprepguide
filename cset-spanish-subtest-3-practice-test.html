CSET Spanish Subtest III: Practice Test & Skills Practice

📋 30 scored questions 🎙 8 speaking prompts ✍️ 8 writing prompts 🆓 100% free 📅 Updated 2026

Subtest III tests four skills — listening, reading, speaking, and writing — so it can't honestly be simulated as a single 100-question multiple-choice quiz. This page gives you real scored practice for the two skills that actually use selected-response questions on the real exam (listening and reading), plus realistic speaking, writing, and constructed-response practice for the two skills that don't.

Actual exam structure (Test Code 147): 30 selected-response questions total — 15 for Listening Comprehension and 15 for Reading Comprehension (Oral Expression and Written Expression have no selected-response questions) — plus 6 constructed-response tasks: 2 short oral responses, 1 short listening response, 1 short reading response, 1 short written response, and 1 extended written response. The exam runs 2 hours 15 minutes and is computer-based only (no online-proctoring option, unlike Subtests I and II). Passing scaled score: 220/300.

Constructed-Response Practice

Listening & Reading: Short Constructed Responses

Beyond the 15+15 selected-response questions, the actual exam also includes one short constructed-response task tied to a separate Listening passage and one tied to a separate Reading passage. Unlike Oral and Written Expression, these may be answered in English or in Spanish.

How Constructed Responses Are Actually Scored

Short (focused) responses (Oral ×2, Listening ×1, Reading ×1, Written ×1) are scored on a 1–3 scale using three performance characteristics:

  • Purpose — how fully the response addresses what the prompt asks
  • Subject Matter Knowledge — accuracy of the content/language knowledge applied
  • Support — quality and specificity of the supporting evidence or examples

The extended written response is scored on a 1–4 scale using those same three characteristics plus a fourth:

  • Depth and Breadth of Understanding — how thoroughly the response demonstrates command of the relevant subject matter

This summary reflects the official CTC/NESINC scoring criteria published in the Subtest III test guide. It is not a proprietary rubric, and no single sample response is "the" only acceptable answer — scorers judge overall effectiveness holistically.

Study Tips for Subtest III

This practice bank cannot be converted into a predicted scaled score — treat it as unofficial study-planning guidance only. Use your results to see which of the four domains needs more attention, not to estimate a pass/fail outcome.

🎧

Practice Real Audio, Not Just Text

This page's listening items use transcripts because there's no audio here. Supplement with real Spanish audio (podcasts, news broadcasts, radio) and practice answering after hearing something only once or twice.

⏱️

Time Your Oral Responses

The real exam gives exactly 2 minutes to plan and 2 minutes to record each oral response, with no replay. Practice under that exact time pressure, out loud, not silently in your head.

✍️

Outline Before You Write

Both written tasks are scored partly on organization. Spend a few minutes outlining — introduction, 2–3 supporting points, conclusion — before drafting, especially for the extended response.

🗣️

Build Idiomatic Range

Oral and Written Expression are both scored partly on command of idiomatic expressions, not just grammatical correctness. Practice using natural Spanish idioms and varied sentence structures, not just textbook-correct sentences.

📖

Read for Structure, Not Trivia

Reading Comprehension tests main idea, inference, tone, purpose, and organization — not literary history facts. Practice identifying an author's implied argument or attitude, not just recalling details.

🌍

Know the Response-Language Rules

Oral and Written Expression responses must be in Spanish. The short constructed responses for Listening and Reading Comprehension may be written in English or Spanish — know this distinction before test day.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does CSET Spanish Subtest III cover?
CSET Spanish Subtest III (Test Code 147) covers four official domains, all under Language and Communication: Oral Expression, Listening Comprehension, Reading Comprehension, and Written Expression. It measures speaking, listening, reading, and writing proficiency in Spanish — not literary history or cultural trivia, which belong to Subtest II.
How many selected-response questions are on Subtest III?
30 total: 15 for Listening Comprehension and 15 for Reading Comprehension. Oral Expression and Written Expression have zero selected-response questions on the actual exam — they're entirely constructed-response.
How many constructed-response tasks are on Subtest III?
Six total: 2 short (focused) oral responses, 1 short listening-comprehension response, 1 short reading-comprehension response, 1 short written response, and 1 extended written response. The exam runs 2 hours 15 minutes and is computer-based only — unlike Subtests I and II, there is no online-proctoring option for Subtest III.
Why does this practice page use transcripts for listening practice?
The actual exam plays audio recordings automatically, usually reading each passage twice, with no ability to pause or replay. This site doesn't have audio, so listening items here are clearly labeled as transcript-based practice — a way to practice the underlying comprehension skills, not a substitute for practicing with real audio.
How should I practice oral expression?
Respond out loud, in Spanish, within the actual exam's timing: 2 minutes to review a prompt and 2 minutes to record your response, with only one attempt. Record yourself and review for vocabulary range, idiomatic expressions, grammatical control, and clear organization, since responses are scored on Purpose, Subject Matter Knowledge, and Support.
How should I practice written expression?
Practice both response types entirely in Spanish: the short (focused) response (roughly 10–15 minutes) and the extended response (roughly 30–45 minutes). Outline your response first, then write for an audience of educators with varied vocabulary and clear organization — the extended response is additionally scored on Depth and Breadth of Understanding.
What is the passing scaled score?
220 out of 300. Because Subtest III mixes selected-response and constructed-response scoring in ways that aren't publicly converted to raw percentages, and because this page's practice format differs from the actual exam's length, no score on this page can be converted into a predicted scaled score — use it for study planning only.
